Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Scoban Insights collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized users connect TikTok accounts to our internal analytics platform.

Last updated: 19 August 2026
On this pageScopeData we accessHow we use dataStorage & retentionSharingSecurityRevocation & deletionContact
01

Scope and who we are

Scoban Insights (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an internal analytics platform operated by Elephant Pal Sdn Bhd for Scoban. It is intended only for authorized personnel and authorized TikTok accounts. This policy applies to information processed through Scoban Insights and its connection to TikTok's developer services.

02

TikTok data we access

Depending on the permissions approved by the account owner and the features enabled by TikTok, we may access:

  • Basic TikTok account information, such as display name, username, avatar, and account identifier.
  • Public or authorized video information, including video identifiers, titles or descriptions, publish dates, thumbnails, and related metadata.
  • Video and account performance metrics, such as views, likes, comments, shares, follower counts, and engagement statistics.
  • OAuth authorization data, including access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the approved connection.
  • Technical and audit information, such as connection status, synchronization time, access logs, and error records.

We do not request TikTok passwords. The exact information available is controlled by TikTok, the permissions requested by the application, and the permissions granted by the account owner.

03

How and why we use data

We process TikTok data only for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • Providing TikTok content analytics and video performance dashboards.
  • Creating internal reports and performance summaries.
  • Supporting authorized Feishu reporting and internal collaboration workflows.
  • Operating, maintaining, troubleshooting, and securing the platform.
  • Complying with applicable law, TikTok's developer terms, and valid legal requests.

We do not use TikTok data for unrelated advertising, independent user profiling, or the sale of personal information.

04

Storage and retention

Information may be stored in access-controlled systems used to operate Scoban Insights and, where enabled, in authorized Feishu workspaces for internal reporting. We retain TikTok data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, meet legitimate operational needs, comply with law, or resolve disputes.

OAuth tokens are retained while the connection is active and are deleted or rendered unusable after revocation or when no longer needed. Cached analytics and reports are periodically reviewed and deleted or anonymized when their business purpose ends, subject to legal or compliance requirements.

05

Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent TikTok data. Information may be disclosed only to:

  • Authorized Scoban personnel who require it for approved internal duties.
  • Service providers that host, secure, or support the platform, under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Feishu, when an authorized workflow sends internal reports to a Scoban-controlled workspace.
  • Regulators, courts, law enforcement, or other parties when required by applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

Service providers may process information only on our instructions and for the services they provide to us.

06

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. These measures include access controls, OAuth-based authorization, restricted credentials, secure transmission where supported, logging, and periodic access review. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

07

Revocation, access, and deletion

An account owner may revoke Scoban Insights' TikTok access through TikTok's account or application settings, where available. Authorized users may also ask us to disconnect an account or delete associated stored data by contacting us below.

After a valid deletion request or revocation, we will delete or de-identify associated data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law, necessary for security or fraud prevention, or preserved in limited backups that cycle out under standard retention schedules. We may need to verify the requester's identity and authority over the relevant account.

08

International processing and children

Information may be processed in countries where Scoban or its service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards. Scoban Insights is a business tool for authorized personnel and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the platform.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the platform, law, or our practices. The “Last updated” date will identify the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate internal channel or on this page.
